# First-round scores against the targets, 10 Oct 2026

The 15 sessions from the route checks (`docs/route-checks.md`), scored with
`tools/score.py` against the targets written afterwards. Indicative only:

- the sessions ran on the seed at b05c307, before the quality bar was in
  `docs/CONVENTIONS.md`, so they were never told about docstrings, strict
  typing or the test bar;
- they were scored with today's `shop/store.py` laid over that seed (it adds
  `fail_write_after` and `rows_read`; no session changed the store);
- one round each, and the full route could not get past its first checkpoint.

| Scenario | Condition | Runs | At target | Behaviour criteria met | Parts not met |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shop-idempotency | bare | 1 | 0 | 8/9 | I9 (a retry scans every order) |
| shop-idempotency | compass | 4 | 0 | 8/9 in all 4 | I9 in all 4; a strict typing error in 2 |
| shop-tracing | bare | 1 | 0 | 6/7 | T6; 4 lint findings (missing docstrings) |
| shop-tracing | compass | 4 | 0 | 5-7/7 | T6 in 3, T2 in 1; 4-5 lint or typing findings in all 4 |
| shop-forget-user | bare | 1 | 0 | 10/11 | F10 (a failed delete at the source still announced) |
| shop-forget-user | compass | 4 | 0 | 0/11 | no code: every run stopped at its first checkpoint |

Coverage of the new code was 100% wherever code was written, and the
tests caught 86-100% of planted bugs, so the tests written in both
conditions were strong. The gaps are in behaviour a production reviewer
would raise (a scan per request, a message with no trace id, an
announcement before the source delete is safe) and in documentation and
typing the sessions were not asked for.

No session reached the target in one round. That is what the multi-round
runner is for: it measures how many rounds, and how much cost, each
condition needs to get there.
